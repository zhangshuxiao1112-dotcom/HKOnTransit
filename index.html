CityPulse Data pipeline

Design
1
Official ETA snapshots

GMB official API: routes, directions, stops, estimates and timestamps.

2
Observed arrivals

Record actual arrivals with synchronised clocks. Exclude and count ambiguous matches.

3
Error analysis and baseline models

Compare official estimates, median residual correction and Ridge regression before adding complexity.

4
Hold-out tests and fallback

Track MAE, predictions within 3 minutes, sample size, source, freshness and availability.
